About US

Infomax is Computer Academy which provides high quality computer education at a very high standard at a low cost. ICA's mission is to create a pool of high quality software professionals who will meet the demanding needs of multinational organizations for tomorrow's challenges. Our courses are designed to provide students with the necessary skills and knowledge to excel in the field of computer science. We offer a wide range of courses, from basic programming to advanced software development, ensuring that our students are well-prepared for their future careers.

Our Mission

We are committed to provide-technology-based education to all sections of the society, for the overall development of the country through continual improvements of quality management system to achieve our objectives of promoting younger generation towards prosperity. We believe that education is the key to success, and we strive to create an environment that fosters learning, creativity, and innovation. Our dedicated team of instructors is passionate about teaching and is always available to support our students in their academic journey.
